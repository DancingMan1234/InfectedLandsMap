Notes & Guides

Tips, loot routes and item info for Infected Lands.

Tips & Tricks

Finding Keycards (Police Armory and Gun Store)

Keep a lookout for the Metal Cabinets, Found in Green Tents and there are lots in Diners around the map. I Have found the keycards very frequently in these.

The Crashed Helicopter event isn't worth it.

The Crashed Helicopter event has a long wait time until the crash, spawns lots of zombies in the open. and only has like 3 green crates and ammo boxes.

Location (Semi) Specific Weapons

While I don't have the exact loot tables, Items like the Sniper Rifle, Mosin, SKS and Shotguns are more frequently found in the Hunting Store. For SMGS and Pistols (also the MP5-SD) are found frequently in the Police Armory.

Weapon Repairs / Quality

Weapon quality can have a serious impact on Damage. If you find something you like, or want to do more damage with the AK. Check Hardware Stores for repair kits. They are pretty frequent. The Improvised "Cleaning" Kits Upgrades it one tier, while the Repair Kit upgrades it two tiers.

Loot Routes

Example: Starter Route Easy

A safe route for new players. About 10 minutes if you take your time.

  1. Milita Base — start here
  2. Checkpoint Delta — Loot Green Crate and Humvey
  3. Overun Checkpoint — Loot all the Metal Cabinets for Keycards
  4. Shopping Center — Loot the Medical Store, Hardware Store for Gun Cleaning Kits, Food Store.
  5. Checkpoint Bravo — Loot the Green Crate in the tent, All of the Metal Cabinets for Keycards
  6. Mayfield Gunstore — Hopefully you would Have the Gun Store Keycard by now. If not there are still plenty of shelves.

Example: High-Risk Route Advanced

Better loot, Longer time, Far more likely to run into people.

  1. Hunting Store - Hopefully you will find the Sniper Rifle or SKS if you are lucky.
  2. Southpoint Gun Store - Might be able to find Rifles, Likely in bad condition though.
  3. Southpoint Hardware Store - Look for Gun Cleaning Kits.
  4. Southpoint Pharmacy - Stock up on Medkits and Antiviral if your a chud and get hit.
  5. Airfield - Check both Hangars, Might find a NG Keycard or some other Weapons.
  6. Evacuation Point / Main Checkpoint - If you do not have any Keycards, Loot the Metal Cabinets.
  7. Louisville Police Station / Gun Store - Depending on what Keycards you manage to find.
